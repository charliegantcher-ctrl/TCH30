How TCH30 works

Membership

TCH30 holds the 30 highest-ranked companies from a 100-stock tech watchlist (semiconductors, AI infrastructure, power and nuclear, space, quantum, robotics, software). Rankings score each company on durability of its 10–15 year role (~30%), growth quality with organic preferred over acquired (~25%), margins and their trend (~20%), free cash flow and balance sheet (~15%), and customer concentration and execution risk (~10%). Share price and valuation play no part in the ranking.

The list is reviewed on the 1st of each month: 10 new companies are researched, the 10 weakest leave the watchlist, and the top 30 is re-ranked. A company enters TCH30 when it ranks in the top 30 and leaves when it falls out.

Weighting

Each member's weight is its share of the 30 members' total market capitalisation, the same way the S&P 500 is weighted. Bigger companies by market value count for more.

Each company page also shows a "business weight" for comparison: its share of the group's trailing-12-month sales, net income (losses count as zero), book value and dividends, equally blended. When a company's market weight is far above its business weight, the market is pricing in a lot of future growth; when it is below, the market is paying less for today's business.

Colors

Green means the figure is positive (growth up, margin above zero, cash flow positive, NRR at or above 100%). Red means negative. Grey means the company does not disclose it or it does not apply, such as NRR for a chipmaker. Operating margin uses the GAAP figure.

Limits

Prices and fundamentals are a snapshot, not live. Quarters differ by company. Figures come from company releases and data aggregators, which companies shape to show themselves well; several figures are computed. This page is for learning and research, not financial advice.
